# State of the art definitions
The state of the art is a focsed synthesis of the current best knowledge and techniques on a research question. 
- Often based on a literature review, but with a review problem-solving orientation
- Highlights slimits, gaps, and open questions
- Recognizing the prior work on a subject
- Avoid unnecessary duplication of work and builds upon existing knowledge
